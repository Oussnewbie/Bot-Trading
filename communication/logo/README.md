# Logo Étrave

Le logo vient du SVG intégré à `etrave/page.html` : une étrave vue de face (une courbe en V et une ligne verticale au centre)
entre deux vagues, en bleu #3987e5. Le nom et le slogan viennent de `etrave/pont.py`.

| Fichier | Usage |
|---|---|
| `etrave_logo.png` | Logo complet sur fond noir (bannière, fin de vidéo) |
| `etrave_icone.png` | Icône seule, 1024 × 1024 |
| `etrave_icone.svg` | Icône en vectoriel, à retoucher ou agrandir sans perte |

## Prompt Nano Banana

Joindre `etrave_logo.png`, puis coller :

```
Refine the attached logo for "ÉTRAVE", an automated trading bot for the New York open. Keep the exact concept: a ship's bow seen head-on (a curved V shape with a vertical center line, the stem) slicing between two small waves. It must stay instantly recognizable.

Improvements:
- Make the bow feel sharper and more dynamic, like it is cutting forward through water — subtle tension in the curves, cleaner proportions.
- Optionally hint at a breakout: the vertical stem can rise slightly above the curve like a price breaking a range.
- Waves: more refined and balanced, slightly lighter weight than the bow.
- Consistent stroke weight, rounded caps, geometric precision, must read clearly at 32px.

Style: minimalist premium fintech emblem, flat vector, no 3D, no gradients, no glossy effects, no extra elements.
Colors: electric blue #3987e5 on near-black #0b0b0b, wordmark in off-white #f0efec.
Typography: "ÉTRAVE" uppercase, bold modern geometric sans-serif, wide letter spacing, keep the accent on the É.

Output: 1:1 square, logo centered on plain #0b0b0b background, high resolution, no mockup, no tagline.
```

Ensuite, dans la même conversation :
- « Icon only, no text, centered, for a TikTok profile picture »
- « Same logo in white on black » pour les vidéos
- « Thicker strokes » si l'icône est trop fine en petit
